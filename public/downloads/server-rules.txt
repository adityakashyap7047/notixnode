================================================================================
MINECRAFT SERVER RULES & COMMUNITY STANDARDS
================================================================================

1. GENERAL CONDUCT
- Treat all community members and staff with respect.
- Zero tolerance for hate speech, harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Doxxing, swatting, or sharing private details results in an instant permanent ban.

2. FAIR PLAY & INTEGRITY
- Any client modifications providing an unfair advantage (x-ray, fly, killaura) are forbidden.
- Duplication glitches and exploit abuse will result in an economy/inventory reset and ban.
- No griefing or stealing within or adjacent to claimed regions.

3. CHAT DISCIPLINE
- No unauthorized advertising of external servers, channels, or services.
- No spamming, flood, or continuous capital letters.
- Follow all directions issued by active staff moderators.
================================================================================
