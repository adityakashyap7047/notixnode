# Minecraft Server Rules & Community Standards
*Authored by NOTIXNODE Technical Resource Team*
*License: CC0-1.0 (Public Domain)*

## 1. Respect & General Conduct
- Treat all players and staff members with courtesy and fairness.
- Harassment, discrimination, hate speech, or real-life threats will result in an immediate and non-appealable ban.
- Doxxing, personal information disclosure, and IP leaking are strictly forbidden.

## 2. Gameplay Integrity
- No hacked clients, unauthorized movement modifications, x-ray packs, or automated macro inputs.
- No item duplication or exploiting engine bugs. Report bugs to staff via ticket immediately.
- Respect town, plot, and region claims. Griefing or stealing from claimed territory is disallowed.

## 3. Communication & Chat
- No advertising of external servers, Discord links, or commercial products.
- Keep chat civil and avoid spamming, caps lock floods, or repetitive character lines.
- Follow staff instructions when directed.
